* A concept for an intelligent framework, based on ideas in the Introduction to Franco ‘Bifo’ Berardi’s Futurability: The Age of Impotence and the Horizon of Possibility

Humans are multi-faceted, complex, messy, emotional, prone to persuasive trickery, and easy to frighten.

In his book, Futurability: The Age of Impotence and the Horizon of Possibility [cite:@berardi2007], Franco ‘Bifo’ Berardi provides a set of five guiding rules that determine how humans behave and make decisions:

- Rational processes of calculation
- Strategic decision
- Ethical judgment
- Aesthetic preference
- Info-psycho stimulation
